Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes uTube for Android (package io.github.omer73364.utube.mobile), maintained by Omar Anwar, and the uTube project website.

Information handled on your device

uTube processes video links you paste or share with the app to retrieve public media information and download the files you select. Download records can include source links, video titles, thumbnails, quality, file locations, timestamps, progress, and error messages. The app stores its queue, history, playlist groups, and preferences locally on your device. Downloaded media is saved to Downloads/uTube.

uTube does not require a uTube account and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not send your download history to a developer-operated account or history service.

Network requests and third-party services

Using a supported link sends requests to the relevant platform (YouTube/Google, X, Instagram, or Facebook) and its media and thumbnail servers. Those services receive your IP address, the requested URL or media identifier, and ordinary connection and request information. Their own privacy policies and logging practices apply. Private or login-restricted downloads are not supported.

The app can contact the uTube website hosted on GitHub Pages to check signed update and service-status information, including periodic checks while the app is in use. App updates you choose to download are obtained from the configured release host, such as GitHub. These hosts receive ordinary network request information, including your IP address. The developer does not control their server logs.

Permissions

  • Internet access retrieves media information, thumbnails, downloads, and update information.
  • Foreground services, wake locks, and notifications support ongoing downloads and show their progress. Notification access is subject to Android settings.
  • Android media storage is used to save downloads. Opening or sharing a saved file gives the selected app access to that file.
  • Permission to install packages supports app updates you request. Android asks for any required installation permission and confirmation.

Retention and deletion

Local history and preferences remain until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Removing a history entry does not delete its downloaded file. Delete media separately using your file manager. Files in shared Downloads storage may remain after app removal. Android backup is disabled in the app manifest; third-party device or file-backup tools may operate independently.

Support and information you choose to share

If you send a support email or open a GitHub issue, the information you provide is received through that service and used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public: avoid posting private links, personal information, credentials, or sensitive logs. You control what you submit.

Website

The website uses browser local storage to remember appearance and language preferences. It does not include advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages and download hosts may process connection information under their own policies. Clear this site's browser data to remove saved preferences.

Children and changes to this policy

uTube is a general-purpose download tool and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account or analytics service. Third-party media may be inappropriate for children. If you believe personal information has been shared with the maintainer in error, contact us. This policy may change as the app changes; the updated policy and date will appear on this page.
